# Profile artwork

`profile.png` is the generated original, and `profile.jpg` is the JPEG export uploaded to Telegram.
The artwork was generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool on 21 October 2026. This is an
independent community project; the artwork contains no official İETT or municipal logo.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create one polished square Telegram bot profile avatar for an independent community Istanbul bus service announcement bot named IETT Notification. Primary subject: a bold friendly yellow Istanbul city bus viewed from the front, combined with a small clear notification bell badge. Style: refined, simple graphic illustration with clean geometric silhouettes, rounded corners and strong contrast, suitable for a tiny circular Telegram avatar. Deep navy backdrop, warm yellow bus, white windshield highlights, restrained turquoise notification accent. Composition centered with generous safe margins for a circular crop; large recognizable bus icon and a compact bell integrated at the upper right of the bus. No lettering, no words, no numbers, no official İETT or municipal logos, no watermark, no photo mockup, no border. Flat solid background, crisp finished app icon, no unnecessary decorative objects.
